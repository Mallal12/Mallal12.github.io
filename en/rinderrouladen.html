Rinderrouladen (German Beef Roulades)

When beef rouladen are braising in the pot, the whole house smells like Sunday. Filled with mustard, bacon, onion and gherkin and slowly cooked in a rich sauce, they turn butter-tender. A festive dish that always goes down well with guests – and is wonderful to prepare ahead.

Preparation30 min Cooking time2 hrs Resting time– Yield4 servings DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Lay out the rouladen on your work surface, pat dry and season both sides with salt and pepper. Spread a thin layer of 1 teaspoon mustard over each inside surface.
  2. Top each roulade with 2 slices of bacon. Arrange onion strips and gherkin sticks at the narrow end, fold in the long sides and roll up firmly. Secure with kitchen twine or roulade pins.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven until very hot and sear the rouladen all over. Remove and set aside.
  4. Brown the carrot, celeriac and remaining onions in the drippings. Add the tomato paste and fry briefly, then deglaze with the red wine and reduce almost completely.
  5. Pour in the stock, add the bay leaf and return the rouladen to the pot. Cover and braise over low heat for about 2 hours, turning the rouladen once.
  6. Remove the rouladen and keep warm. Strain the sauce through a sieve, bring to a boil and thicken slightly with the cornstarch mixture. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Remove the twine or pins and serve the rouladen with the sauce. Red cabbage and potato dumplings or boiled potatoes go beautifully with them.

Tip

Rouladen taste almost even better reheated the next day – ideal when guests are coming and you want to serve without stress. Simply let the meat rest in its sauce.
