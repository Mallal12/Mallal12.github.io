Mandelhörnchen (Almond Crescents)

Moist marzipan crescents rolled in sliced almonds, their tips dipped in dark chocolate – just like from a good bakery, but very easy to make at home.

Preparation25 min Baking time12–15 min Chilling time– Yieldabout 20 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Knead the marzipan with the confectioners' sugar, egg white and ground almonds into a smooth dough. If it is very sticky, chill it briefly.
  2. Shape finger-thick ropes of dough, cut into pieces of about 3 inches (8 cm) and roll them in the sliced almonds.
  3. Bend the pieces into crescents and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–15 minutes, until the almonds are golden. Let cool.
  5. Melt the chocolate and dip the tips of the crescents. Let dry on parchment.

Tip

Moisten your hands slightly while shaping – the marzipan dough will not stick, and the sliced almonds cling better.
