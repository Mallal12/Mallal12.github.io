Elisenlebkuchen (Nuremberg Flourless Lebkuchen)

The queens among gingerbread: made entirely without flour, packed with almonds, hazelnuts and spices, baked on wafers and coated in chocolate.

Preparation30 min Baking time20–25 min Chilling time– Yieldabout 15 cookies (2¾ inch / 70 mm wafers) DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs and sugar with a hand mixer until pale and fluffy.
  2. Fold in the almonds, hazelnuts, candied peels, spice and salt. The mixture is soft – that is exactly right.
  3. Spread the mixture in domes onto the wafers, leaving a small rim free.
  4. Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 20–25 minutes. The lebkuchen should still be moist inside and only lightly colored.
  5. Let cool and coat with melted chocolate, or dip the tops.

Tip

Elisenlebkuchen taste best after 1–2 weeks of storage, when the spices have fully developed.
