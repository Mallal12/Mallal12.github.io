Rindergulasch (German Beef Goulash)

Good goulash needs just two things: good ingredients and a little patience. Plenty of onions and sweet paprika make the sauce thick and rich all by themselves. At our house, goulash is a favorite when the whole family sits around the table – the pot always ends up empty.

Preparation20 min Cooking time2 hrs Resting time– Yield4 servings DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Pat the meat dry. Heat the oil in a heavy pot until very hot and sear the meat in two batches all over. Remove and season with salt.
  2. Fry the onions in the drippings over medium heat for about 10 minutes until dark, stirring often. Briefly fry the garlic and tomato paste with them.
  3. Take the pot off the heat, stir in the paprika and immediately deglaze with the vinegar and stock – this keeps the paprika from turning bitter.
  4. Return the meat with any juices to the pot. Add the bay leaf and marjoram, cover and braise over low heat for about 2 hours, until the meat is butter-tender.
  5. If you like, add the pepper strips for the last 30 minutes. At the end, remove the bay leaf and season the goulash with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve hot – bread dumplings, spaetzle or simply a piece of bread for mopping up the sauce go perfectly with it.

Tip

The onions are the sauce: they break down completely during the long braising and thicken the goulash without any flour. Do not skimp on them – you need at least half as much onion as meat.
