Apfelkuchen (Sunken Apple Cake)

This sunken apple cake is the cake of my childhood: a moist sponge with apple wedges on top that sink in as they bake and turn completely soft. It mixes up quickly, needs no special pan, and tastes wonderful plain, dusted with confectioners' sugar, or still warm with vanilla sauce.

Preparation25 min Baking time45 min Resting time– Yield12 pieces DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease a 10-inch (26 cm) springform pan and dust it with flour, or line the base with parchment.
  2. Peel, quarter and core the apples. Score the rounded side of each quarter several times lengthwise without cutting through, and sprinkle with lemon juice.
  3. Beat the butter, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt until pale and creamy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, about 30 seconds each.
  4. Mix the flour and baking powder and stir in briefly, alternating with the milk, until you have a smooth, spreadable batter.
  5. Fill the batter into the pan and smooth the top. Press the apple quarters, scored side up, tightly side by side into the batter.
  6. Sprinkle with cinnamon if you like and bake in the preheated oven for 40–45 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  7. Let the cake rest in the pan for 10 minutes, then remove it and cool on a wire rack. Dust with confectioners' sugar before serving.

Tip

Tart apples such as Boskoop give the best contrast to the sweet batter. If you like, fold a handful of sliced almonds or raisins into the batter.
