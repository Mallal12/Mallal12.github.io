Cookies Without Egg – 5 Simple Recipes

No egg in the house, an allergy in the family, or simply in the mood for uncomplicated baking? These 5 recipes work completely without egg – and taste so good that nobody will miss anything.

1. Shortcrust Cookies (Basic Recipe)

Knead 2⅓ cups (300 g) flour, 2 sticks (200 g) cold butter, ½ cup + 2 tbsp (100 g) sugar and 1 pinch of salt, chill for 30 minutes, roll out, cut out shapes and bake at 350 °F (180 °C) for 10 minutes.

2. Coconut Macaroons Without Egg

Mix 2⅔ cups (200 g) shredded coconut with ⅔ cup (150 g) sweetened condensed milk, form small mounds and bake at 325 °F (160 °C) for 12–15 minutes until golden.

3. Oatmeal Cookies

Mix 1½ cups (150 g) rolled oats, ¾ cup + 1 tbsp (100 g) flour, 7 tbsp (100 g) melted butter, ⅓ cup + 2 tbsp (80 g) brown sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon, place spoonfuls on the tray and bake at 350 °F (175 °C) for 12 minutes.

4. Peanut Butter Cookies

Stir 1 cup (250 g) peanut butter with ¾ cup (150 g) sugar and 4 tbsp milk, form balls, flatten with a fork and bake at 350 °F (175 °C) for 10 minutes.

5. Chocolate Cookies

Knead 1⅔ cups (200 g) flour, 1⅓ sticks (150 g) butter, ½ cup + 2 tbsp (100 g) sugar, ⅓ cup (30 g) cocoa and 2 tbsp milk into a dough, form rolls, cut slices and bake at 350 °F (180 °C) for 10–12 minutes.

Tip

Doughs without egg are a little more delicate: work with them well chilled and let the cookies cool completely before taking them off the tray.
