Anisplätzchen (Anise Cookies)

These little relatives of springerle grow their typical 'little feet' in the oven and keep a firm cap. The secret: the piped mounds must dry overnight.

Preparation20 min + drying overnight Baking time15–20 min Chilling timedry overnight Yieldabout 40 cookies DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs and confectioners' sugar for at least 10 minutes until pale and thickly creamy – this is the key to the little feet.
  2. Fold in the flour, anise and salt.
  3. Fill the mixture into a piping bag with a plain tip and pipe small mounds of about ¾ inch (2 cm) onto a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Let the cookies dry at room temperature overnight (at least 8 hours), until the surface is matte and firm.
  5. Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 15–20 minutes. The tops stay pale while the little feet form at the base.

Tip

Do not dry them near an open door or in a draft – moving air makes the surface crack.
