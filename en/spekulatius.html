Spekulatius (Spiced Speculoos Cookies)

Crisp, thin and full of warm spices: speculoos tastes of cinnamon, cloves and cardamom. They turn out beautifully even without a wooden mold – simply roll out and cut.

Preparation35 min Baking time10–12 min Chilling time1 hr chilling Yieldabout 45 cookies DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, spice and salt. Quickly knead with the butter, sugar and egg into a smooth dough, wrap and chill for 1 hour.
  2. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to about ⅛ inch (3 mm) thick and cut out any shapes. If you have a speculoos mold, press the dough thinly into the greased, floured mold instead and smooth it off.
  3. Place the cookies on a parchment-lined baking sheet, sprinkle with sliced almonds if you like and press them on lightly.
  4. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 10–12 minutes, until the edges brown slightly. Cool on a wire rack – they crisp up as they cool.

Tip

Speculoos spice is easy to mix yourself: 2 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp each of cloves and nutmeg, and ¼ tsp each of cardamom, ginger and white pepper. The same blend works as gingerbread spice.
