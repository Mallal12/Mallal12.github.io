Klassische Lasagne (Classic Lasagna)

When the lasagna bubbles away in the oven and the kitchen smells of tomato, béchamel and cheese, our whole family gathers round. Layer by layer it becomes a dish that simply always works and leaves everyone full and happy.

Preparation30 min Baking time45 min Resting time10 min resting Yield4 servings DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion and garlic. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and brown the ground beef until crumbly.
  2. Add the onion and garlic and fry briefly. Stir in the tomato paste and let it toast lightly.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and oregano, season with salt and pepper, and simmer the sauce uncovered over medium heat for about 20 minutes.
  4. For the béchamel, melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour and cook briefly until pale. Gradually whisk in the milk, bring to a boil and simmer for about 5 minutes until creamy. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  5. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Spread a thin layer of béchamel over the base of a baking dish (about 8 × 12 inches / 20 × 30 cm).
  6. Layer lasagna sheets, meat sauce and béchamel alternately in the dish, finishing with béchamel. Sprinkle the shredded cheese and Parmesan over the top.
  7. Bake for about 40–45 minutes, until the cheese is golden and the sheets are tender.
  8. Let rest for about 10 minutes before slicing – the pieces will cut much more neatly.

Tip

The top layer of sheets must be completely covered with béchamel, otherwise the edges turn hard. Leftovers reheated the next day taste almost even better.
