Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte (Black Forest Cake)

She is the queen of cakes: chocolate sponge, juicy sour cherries and clouds of whipped cream. A Black Forest cake looks like it comes from a pastry shop, but with a little calm and patience it is very doable at home. For birthdays and guests it is always a little celebration.

Preparation1 hr Baking time25–30 min Chilling time4 hrs chilling Yield12 slices DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line the base of a 10-inch (26 cm) springform pan with parchment; do not grease the sides.
  2. Beat the eggs, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt with a hand mixer for about 8 minutes until pale and thickly creamy. Mix the flour, cocoa and baking powder, sift over the egg mixture and fold in gently.
  3. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 25–30 minutes (test with a skewer). Let the sponge cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out and cool completely. Slice horizontally twice to make 3 layers.
  4. Drain the cherries, reserving the juice. Stir 1 cup (250 ml) cherry juice with the cornstarch and sugar until smooth, bring to a boil and simmer briefly until thickened. Fold in the cherries (set 12 aside for decorating) and let cool.
  5. Whip the cream with the stabilizer and confectioners' sugar until stiff. Sprinkle the bottom layer with half the kirsch, spread the cherry mixture over it and cover with cream.
  6. Place the second layer on top, sprinkle with the remaining kirsch and spread with cream. Add the third layer and cover the whole cake with the remaining cream, reserving a little for piping rosettes.
  7. Sprinkle the cake with chocolate shavings, pipe on 12 cream rosettes and place a cherry on each one.
  8. Chill the cake for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, and serve well chilled.

Tip

The sponge slices best when it is a day old – simply bake it the day before, wrap it in plastic wrap and fill it the next day. A long serrated knife and slow, rotating movements help when cutting the layers.
