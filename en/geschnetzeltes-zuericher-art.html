Geschnetzeltes Züricher Art (Zurich-Style Sliced Veal in Cream Sauce)

This dish is a little celebration for us: tender veal in a creamy sauce with mushrooms, served with soft homemade spaetzle. It looks like a lot of work, but it is on the table faster than you would think.

Preparation25 min Cooking time20 min Resting time– Yield4 servings DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. For the spaetzle, stir the flour, eggs, water, salt and nutmeg into a thick, slow batter and beat until it blisters. Let rest for about 10 minutes.
  2. Bring plenty of salted water to a boil. Press the batter in batches through a spaetzle press or scrape it through a spaetzle maker into the water. As soon as the spaetzle float, lift them out with a slotted spoon, drain and toss in butter. Keep warm.
  3. Slice the mushrooms and finely dice the onion. Pat the meat dry and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Heat the butter in a large pan until very hot and sear the meat in batches for about 2 minutes each. Remove and keep warm.
  5. Fry the mushrooms and onion in the pan juices for about 4–5 minutes. Dust with flour, stir briefly and deglaze with the white wine.
  6. Pour in the broth and cream and simmer the sauce gently for about 5 minutes, until creamy. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Return the meat with any juices to the sauce and warm through briefly – do not let it boil again, so it stays tender.
  8. Sprinkle with parsley and serve together with the spaetzle.

Tip

Always sear the meat in batches in a very hot pan – if too much is in the pan at once, it stews in its own juice instead of browning. To skip the wine, replace it with broth and a squeeze of lemon juice.
