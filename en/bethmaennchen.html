Bethmännchen (Marzipan Almond Balls)

This Frankfurt specialty made of marzipan traditionally carries three almond halves – and is surprisingly easy to make with just a few ingredients.

Preparation25 min Baking time12–15 min Chilling time– Yieldabout 30 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Knead the marzipan with the confectioners' sugar, egg white, ground almonds and flour into a smooth dough. If it is too sticky, work in a little more ground almond.
  2. With slightly damp hands, shape walnut-sized balls and place them on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  3. Press three almond halves, tips up, around each ball.
  4. Whisk the egg yolk with the milk and brush the balls thinly.
  5. Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 12–15 minutes, until lightly golden.

Tip

The almonds stick best if you dip them briefly in the whisked egg yolk before pressing them on.
