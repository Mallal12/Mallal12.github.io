Walnussplätzchen (Walnut Cookies)

Buttery shortcrust with ground walnuts and a walnut half as a crown – rustic, nutty and not too sweet.

Preparation25 min Baking time10–12 min Chilling time30 min chilling Yieldabout 40 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Quickly knead all ingredients (except the walnut halves) into a smooth dough, wrap and chill for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll out the dough to about ⅛ inch (4 mm) thick and cut out circles of about 1¾ inches (4.5 cm).
  3. Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet and press a walnut half lightly into the center of each cookie.
  4. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 10–12 minutes, until the edges take on a little color.
  5. Cool on a wire rack.

Tip

For a chocolate version, dip the bases in dark chocolate coating – walnut and dark chocolate are a perfect pair.
