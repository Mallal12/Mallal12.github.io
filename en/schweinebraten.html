Schweinebraten mit Knödeln (Roast Pork with Bread Dumplings)

A roast pork with crackling skin is the star of any Sunday table. With it come fluffy bread dumplings that soak up the dark gravy beautifully. The recipe takes a little time, but it is not hard at all – perfect when guests are coming.

Preparation25 min Cooking time2 hrs Resting time10 min Yield4 servings DifficultyMedium

Instructions

  1. Score the rind in a diamond pattern with a sharp knife, without cutting into the meat. Rub the meat all over generously with salt, pepper and caraway.
  2. Place the roast rind-side down in a roasting pan, pour in the stock and cook in a preheated oven at 350°F (180°C) for 30 minutes.
  3. Turn the roast over (rind facing up), add the onions and carrot and roast for another 90 minutes. Baste with the pan juices now and then; if the rind darkens too much, cover loosely with foil.
  4. For the dumplings, pour the warm milk over the bread cubes and leave to soak for 15 minutes. Work in the eggs, sweated onion, parsley, salt and nutmeg and shape 8 dumplings with damp hands.
  5. Place the dumplings in plenty of salted water and poach over low heat (do not boil) for about 20 minutes, until they float to the surface.
  6. Take the roast out of the oven and let it rest for 10 minutes. For extra-crisp crackling, switch on the broiler or 425°F (220°C) top heat for the last 5 minutes and watch closely.
  7. Strain the pan juices with the vegetables through a sieve, bring the gravy to a boil, thicken with the cornstarch mixture and season to taste.
  8. Slice the roast and serve with dumplings and gravy. Red cabbage or sauerkraut goes well with it.

Tip

The rind only turns crisp if it goes into the oven dry – do not pour stock over it after scoring, baste only the meat. Leftover raw dumpling mixture freezes well.
