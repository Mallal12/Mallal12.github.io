Kartoffelgratin (Creamy Potato Gratin)

A good potato gratin needs very few ingredients – just thin potato slices, cream, a hint of garlic and time in the oven. It goes with almost everything here, and as a main dish with a salad it makes us just as happy.

Preparation20 min Baking time50 min Resting time10 min resting Yield4 servings DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease a baking dish with butter and rub it with the halved garlic clove.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut or slice them into thin slices of about ⅛ inch (2–3 mm).
  3. Heat the cream and milk in a pan and season generously with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  4. Arrange the potato slices in overlapping layers in the dish and pour the hot cream mixture over them until they are almost covered.
  5. Sprinkle the shredded cheese over the top.
  6. Bake for about 45–50 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden. Test with a knife to check the slices are done.
  7. Let rest for about 10 minutes before serving – the gratin turns beautifully creamy and is easier to portion.

Tip

Evenly thin slices are the most important thing: that way all the potatoes cook at the same time. If the top browns too quickly, simply cover with foil and continue baking.
