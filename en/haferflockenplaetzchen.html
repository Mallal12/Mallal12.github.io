Haferflockenplätzchen (Crisp Oatmeal Cookies)

Golden brown, crisp and warmly spiced with cinnamon: these cookies come together in no time and taste wonderful all year round, not just at Christmas.

Preparation15 min Baking time10–12 min Chilling time– Yieldabout 30 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly. Stir together with the sugar and egg.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt and stir in. Fold in the oats.
  3. Using two teaspoons, place small mounds with space between them on a parchment-lined baking sheet – they spread as they bake.
  4. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are golden brown.
  5. Let firm up briefly on the sheet, then cool on a wire rack – that is how they turn out beautifully crisp.

Tip

Swap ½ cup (50 g) of the oats for chopped hazelnuts, or fold in a handful of chocolate chips – both taste wonderful.
