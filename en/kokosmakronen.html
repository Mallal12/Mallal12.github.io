Kokosmakronen (Coconut Macaroons)

Crisp at the edges, wonderfully moist inside: coconut macaroons need only a handful of ingredients and are perfect when time is short.

Preparation15 min Baking time18–20 min Chilling time– Yieldabout 35 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Beat the egg whites with the salt until stiff. Gradually sprinkle in the sugar and vanilla sugar and keep beating until glossy.
  2. Carefully fold in the shredded coconut with a spatula.
  3. Arrange the wafers on a parchment-lined baking sheet and pile small mounds of the coconut mixture onto each with two teaspoons.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven at 300°F (150°C) for 18–20 minutes, until the tips are lightly golden.
  5. Let cool. If you like, dip the bases in melted chocolate.

Tip

Do not overbake – the macaroons should stay moist inside and will firm up a little more as they cool.
