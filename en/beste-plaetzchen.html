The 10 Best Christmas Cookie Recipes 2026

The same question every year: which cookies go into the tin? Here are the 10 recipes our readers love the most – from timeless classics to quick last-minute ideas.

CategoryChristmas cookies Collection10 recipes Rangeeasy to festive

  2. How to plan your baking session

The Top 10

  1. Vanillekipferl – tender, crumbly, with real vanilla. The undisputed number 1.
  2. Zimtsterne – moist center and white glaze, completely flourless.
  3. Butter cookies for cutting out – the basic recipe for decorating with the whole family.
  4. Spitzbuben – shortcrust sandwiches with redcurrant jelly and confectioners' sugar.
  5. Lebkuchen – soft, spiced and a poem with chocolate glaze.
  6. Spekulatius – crisp and full of Christmas spices.
  7. Schwarz-Weiß-Gebäck – the pretty checkerboard pattern from light and dark dough.
  8. Kokosmakronen – only 4 ingredients, crisp outside, moist inside.
  9. Heidesand – fine sandy shortbread that melts on the tongue.
  10. Cookies without egg – when the egg is missing or allergies play a role.

How to Plan Your Baking Session

Start 2–3 weeks before Christmas with the varieties that keep well (Vanillekipferl, Heidesand, Spekulatius) and bake the delicate ones last. In tins, separated by variety with parchment paper in between, cookies stay fresh for 3–4 weeks.
