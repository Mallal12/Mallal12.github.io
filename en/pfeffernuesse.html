Pfeffernüsse (Spiced Pepper Nut Cookies)

Small, round and full of warm spice: pepper nuts get their special note from honey, gingerbread spice and a pinch of real pepper – do not worry, they do not taste hot.

Preparation30 min Baking time12–15 min Chilling time30 min chilling Yieldabout 50 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Melt the honey and sugar in a pan over low heat, then let cool slightly.
  2. Mix the flour, baking soda, spice blend and pepper. Add the honey mixture and the egg and knead into a firm dough.
  3. Wrap the dough and chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Shape small balls of about ¾ inch (2 cm), place on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake at 350°F (180°C) for 12–15 minutes.
  5. Stir the confectioners' sugar with the water into a thick glaze and coat the still-warm cookies with it. Let dry.

Tip

Pepper nuts are quite hard when fresh – after 2–3 days in the tin with a piece of apple, they turn beautifully soft.
