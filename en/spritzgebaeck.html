Spritzgebäck (Piped Butter Cookies)

Delicate piped butter cookies belong in every well-filled cookie tin. With a cookie press or piping bag, the pretty sticks, wreaths and S-shapes almost make themselves.

Preparation25 min Baking time10–12 min Chilling time30 min chilling Yieldabout 45 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Beat the butter, sugar and vanilla sugar with a hand mixer until pale and creamy. Beat in the egg.
  2. Mix the flour, salt and almonds and knead briefly into the butter mixture to form a smooth, soft dough.
  3. Fill a cookie press or a piping bag with a star tip and pipe sticks, wreaths or S-shapes onto a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Chill the piped cookies for 30 minutes – this keeps their shape in the oven.
  5. Bake in a preheated oven at 350°F (180°C) for 10–12 minutes until pale; they should barely color.
  6. Once cool, dip the ends in melted chocolate if you like and let them set.

Tip

If the dough is too stiff to pipe, stir in 1–2 tablespoons of milk. Too soft? A short rest in the fridge fixes it.
