Heidesand (Brown Butter Shortbread Slices)

Their secret is browned butter: these slice-and-bake shortbread cookies taste delicately of caramel, are incredibly crumbly, and are simply cut from a log – no rolling or cutting out.

Preparation20 min Baking time12–15 min Chilling time2 hrs chilling Yieldabout 50 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a pan and let it brown lightly over medium heat until it smells nutty. Pour into a bowl and let it cool until set but still spreadable.
  2. Cream the browned butter with the sugar and vanilla sugar. Knead in the flour and salt.
  3. Shape the dough into two logs of about 1½ inches (4 cm) in diameter, roll them in sugar, wrap and chill for at least 2 hours.
  4. Cut the logs into slices about ¼ inch (5 mm) thick and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–15 minutes, until the edges just begin to color. Lift off carefully – they are very fragile while hot.

Tip

Heidesand tastes best on the second day. In a well-sealed tin it keeps for 3–4 weeks.
