Käsespätzle (Cheese Spaetzle)

Cheese spaetzle is pure comfort food for us: soft spaetzle, melting mountain cheese in between, and crisp roasted onions on top. Add a green salad – and that is all you need for a cozy evening.

Preparation20 min Cooking time20 min Resting time– Yield4 servings DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Stir the flour, eggs, water, salt and nutmeg into a thick, slow batter and beat until it blisters. Let rest for about 10 minutes.
  2. Slice the onions into thin rings and roast them slowly in the butter over medium heat for about 10 minutes until golden brown. Set aside.
  3. Bring plenty of salted water to a boil. Press the batter in batches through a spaetzle press or scrape it through a spaetzle maker into the water.
  4. As soon as the spaetzle float, lift them out with a slotted spoon and drain well.
  5. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Layer spaetzle and cheese alternately in a greased baking dish, seasoning lightly with salt and pepper.
  6. Bake for about 8–10 minutes, until the cheese has melted and pulls into strings.
  7. Serve sprinkled with the roasted onions and chives – a green salad goes perfectly with it.

Tip

For extra creamy cheese spaetzle, add 2–3 tablespoons of hot spaetzle water between the layers. A mix of two cheeses – one mild and one flavorful – gives the best taste.
