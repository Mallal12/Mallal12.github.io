Weiche Lebkuchen (Soft Gingerbread Rounds)

Moist, soft gingerbread rounds just like at the Christmas market – with honey, brown sugar and plenty of gingerbread spice. They stay soft in the tin for weeks.

Preparation25 min Baking time12–15 min Chilling time30 min chilling Yieldabout 25 cookies DifficultyEasy

Instructions

  1. Melt the honey, sugar and butter in a pan and let cool slightly.
  2. Mix the flour, gingerbread spice and baking soda. Stir in the honey mixture and the egg and knead into a soft dough. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Using two tablespoons, place mounds of dough on a parchment-lined baking sheet and flatten slightly – the dough spreads a little as it bakes, so leave space.
  4. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 12–15 minutes. The gingerbread may still feel soft in the middle.
  5. Stir the confectioners' sugar with the lemon juice until smooth and brush the cooled rounds with it.

Tip

A piece of apple or a slice of bread in the cookie tin keeps gingerbread soft for a long time – replace it every few days.
