Hi, I'm Anna!

Great to have you here! I'm the cook, baker and recipe collector behind Lecker Rezepte. The recipes you find here are created in my kitchen – simple, honest and always with the goal that they turn out just as well for you as they do for me.

How it all began

My love of baking comes from my grandma. As a child I stood on a small stool at her kitchen counter and was allowed to roll the Vanillekipferl in confectioners' sugar – of course, more was nibbled than rolled. I still guard her handwritten recipe book like a treasure today.

Over the years, Grandma's book grew into a whole collection: family recipes, classics from old cookbooks and new ideas that I test until they are truly reliable. Those are exactly the recipes I share with you here.

What you will find here

  • Reliable recipes: Every step is explained so that even beginners can bake along with confidence.
  • Honest tips: No embellished information – if a dough needs resting time, it says so.
  • Recipes with a story: Many dishes come from our family and have been prepared this way for generations.

My favorite recipes

  • Vanillekipferl, just like Grandma's – my absolute Christmas classic
  • Zimtsterne – moist, cinnamon-y and beautiful
  • Butter cookies for cutting out – the recipe everything started with

You can also find me on Pinterest

On Pinterest I collect all recipes as pins to save and bake later – feel free to stop by and follow me there so you never miss a new recipe.
